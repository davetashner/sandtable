The map has changed scale by three orders of magnitude and the story has not moved. Everything on this chapter's map is within an hour's walk of everything else.

That is worth dwelling on, because it explains the shape of the February days better than any account of intentions does. Petrograd is a city built across a river delta, and in 1917 the working districts — Vyborg to the north, Narva and the [Putilov works](place:putilov-works) to the south-west — were separated from the government quarter by water. The authorities' plan for civil disorder was accordingly a plan about bridges. On 24 February the crowds walked over the ice, because it was February and the Neva was a road.

Look at where the days go. The strike starts in the mills at the top of the map. It reaches the [Nevsky Prospekt](place:znamenskaya-square) — the one street in the city everybody uses — on the second day, and after that the demonstration is no longer in the workers' districts but in the government's. On the fourth day the shooting happens on the Nevsky and on Znamenskaya Square, in front of the shops, in the middle of the afternoon.

And then the crucial fact of the geography, which is that the regiments were quartered in the middle of the city rather than outside it. The reserve battalions of the Guards were in barracks on the Liteiny and around the Tauride, packed four and five thousand men into buildings meant for a fraction of that, next door to the streets they had been ordered to clear.[^hasegawa-1981] They had watched the crowds for three days. They had been fired on by nobody. On the night of 26 February they went back to those barracks and argued, and in the morning the argument came out into the street.

A garrison outside the city might have held. A garrison inside it, made of the wrong men — conscripts and recovered wounded rather than the regiments that had gone to the front in 1914 — was not a garrison at all; it was part of the crowd that happened to be indoors.[^figes-1996]

By the evening of 27 February the only building on this map that still mattered was the [Tauride Palace](place:tauride-palace), where two bodies were forming in two wings. Everything else had already been decided by people walking.
