The same map as February, eight months later, and almost none of it is contested.

What you are looking at is a list of buildings. Bridges, the central telegraph, the telephone exchange, the state bank, the post office, the power station, the four railway termini, the printing works. Not one of them is a strongpoint and not one of them was fought over. Detachments walked up, told the guard it was relieved, and were believed — because the guard had been taking its orders from the Military Revolutionary Committee for the better part of a week already.

The thing that makes this look like an operation is [Smolny](place:smolny-institute), three kilometres east of the centre, where a committee sat with a list and a telephone. The thing that makes it *not* an operation is that most of the list had been ticked before the night began.

Two points on the map are worth arguing with.

The first is the [Winter Palace](place:winter-palace). It is the only place on this chapter's map where anybody had to be persuaded by force, and even there the persuasion was mostly a matter of waiting: the cadets, the women's battalion and the bicycle troops leaked away through the evening while the attackers leaked in through the side doors, and the ministers were arrested in a dining room at about ten past two in the morning without a fight in the building.[^rabinowitch-1976] The plan for taking it was a poor one and almost every part of it went wrong. It made no difference, because nothing was resisting.

The second is the [Mariinsky Palace](place:mariinsky-palace), where the Provisional Council of the Republic — the Pre-Parliament, three weeks old — was surrounded at midday and told to go home, and did.

That is the whole of it, and it is why the argument about October is not a military argument. Nothing on this map was captured. The capital was transferred, in a week of paperwork, from a government that could not give an order to a committee that could, and the buildings changed hands afterwards because buildings follow orders.[^figes-1996]
