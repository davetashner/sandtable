[Lanrezac](person:lanrezac-charles) has gained his day; on the morning of the 30th he disengages on [Joffre](person:joffre-joseph)'s order and the 5th Army falls back on Laon. [Bülow](person:bulow-karl-von), checked on the Oise with a battered right, asks the 1st Army to wheel inward to help him exploit the 'victory' — and [Kluck](person:kluck-alexander-von), who has already been pulled back east by Bülow once, turns his army south-east towards the Oise and the Marne, inside Paris instead of around it.[^herwig-2009]

That is why this small battle matters: the counter-attack at Guise is the operational cause of the turn that exposed the right wing's flank to Paris and made the Marne possible. Lanrezac was relieved on 3 September anyway — for the retreat from Charleroi and for what Joffre saw as defeatism — and Franchet d'Espèrey, who had won the afternoon, was given his army. Whether Lanrezac was a scapegoat or a spent commander is argued; that Guise bought the time the redeployment needed is not.[^herwig-2009] [^tuchman-1962] [^afgg-1-1]
