[Kluck](person:kluck-alexander-von) is winning on the Ourcq and losing the battle. With the III and IX Corps also ordered north in the night, only Marwitz's cavalry and a few Jäger battalions stand between his army and [Bülow](person:bulow-karl-von)'s — a gap thirty kilometres wide by evening, into which the BEF and the 5th Army are walking: the British reach Coulommiers and the Grand Morin, Franchet d'Espèrey's left the approaches to Montmirail.[^herwig-2009] [^edmonds-1922]

[Maunoury](person:maunoury-michel-joseph), hard pressed at Étrépilly and on his left at Nanteuil, asks Paris for everything it has. The IV Corps, railed up from the 3rd Army, is coming; [Gallieni](person:gallieni-joseph) also requisitions the city's taxis, and through the night some six hundred of them carry part of the 7th Division out to Nanteuil and Silly-le-Long in two runs. Militarily it is a few thousand men; in memory it is the battle.[^tuchman-1962] [^afgg-1-1]
