The Kaiser comes to Rupprecht's headquarters to be present when Nancy falls. There is an escort ready and a route into the city. It is the clearest picture in the campaign of what the war was still expected to look like: a sovereign riding into a captured provincial capital, the recovered Lorraine, a scene from 1870.[^herwig-2009]

The assault on 7 September, against Sainte-Geneviève on the northern hinge above the Moselle, goes in piecemeal and fails. Nothing is taken. The Kaiser leaves without his entry.[^afgg-1-1]

Behind the theatre, OHL has already begun to take the decision away: the reinforcements that were to widen this offensive are being ordered west, where the gap between Kluck and Bülow is opening. Lorraine had been the place where Moltke thickened the left because he could not bear to give ground there. In the first week of September the thickened left is spending its strength on a hill above Nancy while the wheel it was supposed to make possible comes apart on the Marne.[^tyng-1935] [^herwig-2009]
