On the evening of 12 August the first 42 cm shell falls on Fort de Pontisse from Mortier, east of the ring; the fort is a wreck by morning and surrenders at midday. Embourg and Chaudfontaine follow on the 13th — Chaudfontaine's magazine explodes — Liers and Fléron on the 14th, Boncelles and Lantin on the 15th. Concrete built against the 21 cm shell of 1888 is opened like a tin by the 42 cm shell of 1914.[^herwig-2009] [^strachan-2001]

At twenty past five on 15 August a shell reaches Loncin's magazine. The fort goes up with most of its garrison; [Leman](person:leman-gerard) is dug from the ruins unconscious and carried to Emmich, who returns his sword. The same evening, at Visé on the river, the town is burned and its people deported — part of the August killings Horne and Kramer trace to the German army's panic about civilian snipers.[^tuchman-1962] [^horne-kramer-2001]
