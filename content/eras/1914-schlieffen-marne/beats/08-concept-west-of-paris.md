**Hypothetical.** Here the memorandum's drawing and the historical map part company. On 30–31 August the real [Kluck](person:kluck-alexander-von) turned south-east to roll up the French left; on this map the 1st Army holds its course, crosses the Oise and the Seine below Paris and passes the capital on the outside, the 2nd Army keeping station inside it and the 3rd pinning the centre from Reims and Châlons.[^herwig-2009]

[Schlieffen](person:schlieffen-alfred-von)'s reasoning for the outside line was the pivot: an army turning _inside_ Paris drives the French armies towards their own fortresses and reserves; an army passing _outside_ it can turn east behind them and pin them against that line. The memorandum itself asked for more divisions than the army possessed to do it.[^zuber-2002] [^mombauer-2001]
