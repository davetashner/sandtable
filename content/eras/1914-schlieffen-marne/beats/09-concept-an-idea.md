**Hypothetical.** The wheel is complete on the map and the branch stops there: it shows where the memorandum wanted the right wing to be, and the distance between that line and the Aisne, where the real right wing stood on these dates.[^herwig-2009]

It cannot show whether the march was possible, because that depends on things a drawing leaves out — corps that were sent elsewhere, railways that were blown, an enemy who redeployed by train. Those are scored, one by one, in the branch 'What success required'; the question of whether there was ever a plan to fail is the first entry in this pack's historiography.[^zuber-2002] [^mombauer-2001]
