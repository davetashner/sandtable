[Joffre](person:joffre-joseph)'s order of the day reaches the armies at dawn: the battle on which the country's safety depends has begun; there will be no looking back; troops which can no longer advance are to hold their ground and die where they stand.[^afgg-1-1]

From the Ourcq to Verdun the whole [French](person:french-john) line turns. The BEF, slow and unconvinced, comes forward from Tournan and Rozay towards the Grand Morin; Franchet d'Espèrey's 5th Army attacks [Kluck](person:kluck-alexander-von)'s III and IX Corps at Esternay and the Saxons and the Guard close on Foch at the marshes of Saint-Gond.[^edmonds-1922] [^afgg-1-1] On the Ourcq the II Corps arrives at Lizy and Trilport by noon and goes straight into the fight. Kluck now has two corps facing Paris and two facing south; in the night he orders a third, the IV, to march north as well.[^herwig-2009]
