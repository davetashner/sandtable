**This branch is hypothetical — an argument, not a prediction.** For the envelopment to work, 25 August has to go differently: no Guard Reserve and XI Corps entrained for East Prussia; no corps left before Antwerp and Maubeuge; the railheads keeping pace through a Belgium whose bridges and tunnels are intact. On this map the 1st and 2nd Armies march with the weight they had on 17 August.[^herwig-2009]

And the French have to fail to do the one thing Joffre actually did that day: Instruction générale no 2 — the retreat, and the new army built at Amiens from corps railed out of Lorraine. In this branch the trains do not run west in time, and the left of the French line ends at Lanrezac's 5th Army.[^afgg-1-1] Each of those conditions is on the checklist above, scored against what happened.
