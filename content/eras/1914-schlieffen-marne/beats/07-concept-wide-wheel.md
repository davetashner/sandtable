**This branch is hypothetical.** It draws the sweep as the 1905 memorandum imagined it: the 1st Army, at full strength, keeps swinging west and south through Amiens, passing *outside* Paris rather than turning short of it — using the fortified capital as a pivot to trap the retreating French armies against their own fortress line instead of driving them toward it.[^herwig-2009]

Whether any such plan existed as a plan is argued. Zuber holds that the memorandum was a staff study and that "the Schlieffen Plan" was constructed after the war to explain defeat;[^zuber-2002] Mombauer and others reply that Moltke inherited and modified a real operational concept.[^mombauer-2001] The branch's feasibility checklist lists what would have had to be true for the wheel to work — and marks which of those things actually were.
