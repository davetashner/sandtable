**Hypothetical.** On paper the campaign ends here: the 5th, 9th and 4th Armies caught between Troyes and the fortress line, the BEF gone behind the Seine, a decisive envelopment before Russia's mobilization is complete, and the armies entrain for the East.[^strachan-2001] That was the war Germany believed it could not otherwise win on two fronts.

Compare it with the historical branch on the same date: the same armies, the same six weeks, and the right wing on the Aisne. The distance between the two maps is the argument of this pack — a timetable the world fell behind — and the checklist is its evidence: seven things had to be true, and none of them was.[^herwig-2009] [^strachan-2001]
