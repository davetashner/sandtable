French Plan XVII throws the 1st and 2nd Armies into Lorraine on 14 August, straight at the German left — very nearly the trap Schlieffen had imagined. Rupprecht's 6th Army absorbs the blow and counterattacks at Morhange–Sarrebourg on 20 August; the French fall back on Nancy and the Meurthe.[^afgg-1-1] The 3rd and 4th Armies attack into the Ardennes on 21–23 August and collide head-on with the German 4th and 5th in fog and forest, with the worst French losses of the war.[^herwig-2009]

On the Allied left, meanwhile, the German right wing arrives. Bülow forces the Sambre at Charleroi (21–23 August) and Hausen the Meuse at Dinant; Lanrezac's 5th Army, in danger of envelopment, retreats on 23 August without telling the British on its left, who fight the 1st Army at Mons that same day and then must retreat too.[^edmonds-1922] By 24 August every Allied army from Belgium to the Vosges is falling back.
