On 25 August Moltke took two corps off the right wing and sent them east. Namur had fallen, the French were retreating on every front, and from Luxembourg the war in the West looked finished; the war in the East looked lost. The Guard Reserve Corps and the XI Corps, with the 8th Cavalry Division, entrained the next day for East Prussia.[^herwig-2009] [^mombauer-2001]

Nobody in East Prussia had asked for them. The crisis that prompted the order had already been answered by relieving the man who raised it, and the new command was busy doing the opposite of retreating: screening Rennenkampf, moving the rest of the army across the front of one Russian army to fall on the other. Between 26 and 30 August the ring closed on Samsonov's Second Army and some 92,000 prisoners were taken. In the early hours of the 30th Samsonov walked into the forest near Willenberg and shot himself.[^showalter-2004] [^stone-1975]

The two corps spent the battle on trains.
