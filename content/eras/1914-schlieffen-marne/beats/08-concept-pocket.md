**Hypothetical.** With the right wing sweeping south of Paris and the left wing holding firm on the Metz hinge, the retreating French armies find their line of retreat cut. The concept's logic — Schlieffen's borrowed Cannae — is a double envelopment: pin the centre, encircle with the wings.[^herwig-2009]

The same sources that describe the idea describe why it strained belief: the outer wing would have had to march farther and faster than the historical 1st Army already did, on roads and railways the Belgians had wrecked, with corps that had in fact been sent elsewhere, while Joffre — in the concept — fails to do the one thing he actually did, which was to redeploy west by rail.[^zuber-2002]
