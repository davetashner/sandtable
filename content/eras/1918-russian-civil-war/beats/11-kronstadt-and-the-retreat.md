The last White army had been gone for three months when the government nearly
fell.

The winter of 1920–21 was the worst of the whole period: industry had
effectively stopped, the towns were being abandoned by their own populations,
and the requisitioning went on because the alternative was that the towns
starved. Western Siberia rose in December and cut the Trans-Siberian. Tambov had
been in arms since August. In February the Petrograd factories struck, and the
government put the city under martial law and sent in the officer cadets.

Then the sailors of [Kronstadt](place:kronstadt) — the garrison that had been
the hardest core of October, the one Trotsky had once called the pride and glory
of the revolution — held a mass meeting of fifteen thousand on Anchor Square on
1 March and passed a resolution.

Read the [fifteen
demands](1918-russian-civil-war:document-petropavlovsk) as a description of
what War Communism looked like from underneath. New soviet elections by secret
ballot. Freedom of the press for workers and peasants and for anarchist and left
socialist parties. Release of socialist political prisoners. A commission to
review the cases of those held "in prisons and concentration camps". The
abolition of the road-block detachments that searched passengers on trains for
food. Equal rations. And the peasant's right to his own land and his own cattle,
"on condition that the peasants manage with their own means, that is, without
employing hired labor".[^avrich-1973]

Nothing in that list is counter-revolutionary unless the revolution is defined
as the party. That is what the argument was about, and it is why the government
could not concede it and survive as itself.

The fortress was taken across the ice on the night of 16–17 March, by picked
units in white robes, after three failed assaults. Three hundred delegates were
sent from the Tenth Party Congress, sitting in Moscow, to stiffen the attack.
Afterwards, in Chamberlin's sentence, "the Cheka did not emulate the humanity of
the Kronstadt insurgents, who spared all their Communist
prisoners".[^chamberlin-1935-2]

## And on 8 March, Lenin conceded

While the guns were firing, the Tenth Congress heard Lenin announce the New
Economic Policy; on 16 March a decree replaced the requisitioning of the
peasants' produce with a fixed tax in kind, everything above which was the
peasant's to sell.[^chamberlin-1935-2] That is the sailors' eleventh demand,
granted two days before the men who made it were killed for making it.

Whether Kronstadt caused the retreat or merely dated it is
[argued](1918-russian-civil-war:link-kronstadt-to-nep) — the crisis was general
and the change had been under discussion before March. What is not arguable is
that the concession was made to peasants with rifles in their hands, and that
the men who made it in 1921 were mostly the men who took it back in 1929.
